## Limitations

- A Git commit date or repository creation date is not, by itself, conclusive proof of authorship or legal ownership.
- A cryptographic hash proves file identity relative to a known digest; it does not prove who created the file.
- A transparency-log entry can support evidence that a digest/signing event existed by a time, but it does not grant copyright, trademark, patent rights, or worldwide exclusivity.
- Copyright generally protects qualifying expression (such as source code), not an abstract idea, method, product name, or business concept as such.
- Trademark and patent rights depend on jurisdiction, eligibility, prior rights, procedure, and sometimes fees. No registration or filing is claimed by this document.
